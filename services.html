Review Replies

Every new Google review answered

$49/mo

Included in Starter, Growth and Pro

You get

  • Each week we read your new Google reviews and write a reply in your voice
  • 4- and 5-star replies post automatically; 1- to 3-star replies are approved by a person first
  • Replies never invent prices, hours or offers, and never promise refunds or discounts
  • Replies you wrote yourself are never overwritten
  • A weekly summary email: new reviews, replies and your rating trend

You provide

How it’s delivered

Your first week covers reviews from the last 30 days.

Accessibility Monitoring

A monthly accessibility check of your site

$39/mo

Included in Starter, Growth and Pro

You get

  • A monthly scan of up to 10 pages of your website for common accessibility problems: images without descriptions, unlabeled form fields, heading order, unclear links, missing page language and more
  • A plain-language report with issues ranked by importance and the fix for each
  • A month-over-month trend so you can see progress

You provide

Your website address, entered on your plan settings page after checkout.

How it’s delivered

Sent automatically each month. Automated checks can’t judge everything (for example colour contrast or keyboard use), and the report is not a guarantee of legal compliance.
