Menu Care

Unlimited menu updates, same QR code

…

You get

  • Send changes any time from your private change link: dishes, prices, sections, specials
  • Each update is checked against your request and approved by us before it goes live
  • Your menu stays at the same address, so printed QR codes keep working
  • A monthly email with one seasonal special idea built from your own menu
  • Hosting for your Menu-Made menu included

You provide

Your changes, written the way you would tell a server. Works with a menu hosted by Menu-Made: start with Online Menu + QR if you don’t have one yet.

How it’s delivered

Every change is reviewed before it is published.

Review Replies

Every new Google review answered

…

You get

  • Each week we read your new Google reviews and write a reply in your voice
  • 4- and 5-star replies post automatically; 1- to 3-star replies are approved by a person first
  • Replies never invent prices, hours or offers, and never promise refunds or discounts
  • Replies you wrote yourself are never overwritten
  • A weekly summary email: new reviews, replies and your rating trend

You provide

Connect your Google Business Profile once from the link we email you. Optional: tone, sign-off, how unhappy guests can reach you, topics to avoid.

How it’s delivered

Your first week covers reviews from the last 30 days.

Google Profile Care

A busy, up-to-date Google profile

…

You get

  • A new Google post every week, written from your specials and profile, with a photo from your library
  • Up to 3 photos from your library added to your profile each month
  • Review replies, the same way as Review Replies (included)
  • A monthly report: views in Search and Maps, calls, direction requests and website clicks, compared with the month before
  • A weekly email showing what went live

You provide

Connect your Google profile once; add photos to your photo library; tell us your specials when you have them (optional).

How it’s delivered

Posts are checked so they only state facts you gave us; anything that fails the check is held back, not published.

Accessibility Monitoring

A monthly accessibility check of your site

…

You get

  • A monthly scan of up to 10 pages of your website for common accessibility problems: images without descriptions, unlabeled form fields, heading order, unclear links, missing page language and more
  • A plain-language report with issues ranked by importance and the fix for each
  • A month-over-month trend so you can see progress

You provide

Your website address, entered on your plan settings page after checkout.

How it’s delivered

Sent automatically each month. Automated checks can’t judge everything (for example colour contrast or keyboard use), and the report is not a guarantee of legal compliance.

Guest App

Your own app on guests’ phones

… + … setup

You get

  • An app guests add to their home screen from your link or QR code: menu, specials, hours, location, call button and your ordering or reservation link

Online Menu + QR

Your menu as a mobile page, plus a QR code for every table

… first year, up to 3 pages

You get

  • A mobile menu page built from the PDF or photos of your current menu
  • Your logo and colours (optional)
  • A printable QR code that opens it
  • First year of hosting and two revisions included

You provide

Your current menu as a PDF or photos, uploaded at checkout. That’s all we need before payment.

How it’s delivered

After the first year, hosting continues with Menu Care, which also keeps your menu up to date; without Menu Care the menu goes offline 30 days after the first year (we remind you before).
