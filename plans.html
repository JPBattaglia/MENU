Billing questions

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Every plan email has a link to your billing page, where you can switch to another plan, move between monthly and yearly billing, update your card, see invoices and cancel. Switching takes effect right away and is prorated; cancelling stops the plan at the end of the period you’ve paid for.

What is the setup fee?

A one-time $149 to build your menu from your PDF or photos, in the design you pick, with its link, QR code and print files. It is free with MΞNU Max.

What if I cancel?

Your plan runs to the end of the period you’ve paid for. Your menu stays online for 30 more days, then goes offline; nothing is deleted, and starting a plan again brings it back at the same link and QR code.

Monthly or yearly?

Either. Yearly plans cost 10 times the monthly price, so you get 2 months free.
