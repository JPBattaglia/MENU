Billing questions

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Every plan email has a link to your billing page, where you can switch to another plan, move between monthly and yearly billing, update your card, see invoices and cancel. Switching takes effect right away and is prorated; cancelling stops the plan at the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Is the free menu really free?

Yes, for as long as you keep it: your menu online in the Classic design with its own link and QR code, and you change it yourself. It shows a small “Made with MΞNU” badge. Add MΞNU Plus any time and your link and QR code stay the same.

What if I stop paying for Plus or MΞNU Premium?

Your menu stays online at the same link and QR code on the free plan, with the badge, and without the print set or translations.

Monthly or yearly?

Either. Yearly plans cost 10 times the monthly price, so you get 2 months free.
