A few things before you start

Clear scope, clear pricing, and a menu that feels like your business.

What do I receive?

A branded online menu of up to 3 pages and a ready-to-share QR code, with two revisions included. Send your current menu, business name, logo, colors, contact email, and destination link.

What will I pay today?

$144 for the first year: $129 setup plus $15 hosting. Each page beyond the first 3 adds $20. Choose your page count at checkout to see the full total before paying.

When will my menu be ready?

Standard delivery is within 48 hours after complete menu materials are received and validated. Missing materials and custom requests require an agreed delivery date.

What do the two revisions cover?

Two revisions to your menu are included in the package. Ask about larger changes before purchasing so we can confirm the scope. Future edits and work beyond the included revisions are quoted before work begins.

What happens to hosting after the first year?

Hosting after year one costs $15/year and is arranged separately. This checkout is a one-time payment and does not create an automatic subscription.

Can I update prices or dishes later?

Yes. Send the changes you need and we’ll confirm the scope and price before making them. Ongoing edit fees are quoted individually.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. The online menu opens in a web browser from its link or QR code. A dedicated native app is a separate optional service.
