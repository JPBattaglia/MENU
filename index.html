A few things before you start

Choose the right service, understand the scope, and know what happens next.

Can you build a new website or improve my existing one?

Both. New websites and larger redesigns are scoped and quoted individually. The $299 Website Conversion Fix covers targeted improvements to an existing site’s clarity, calls to action, mobile usability, and page flow.

Do I need a menu package to use the other services?

No. Website work, Google Visibility, Accessibility Upgrade, and app projects can be discussed independently. Choose the service your business needs.

How much do the services cost?

Website Conversion Fix is $299, Google Visibility is $149, and Accessibility Upgrade is $250 for one year. Online Menu + QR starts at $144 for the first year. The menu app scope is $999. Custom websites and work beyond these scopes are quoted separately.

What if I need a custom project?

Send a project inquiry with your goals, current website if you have one, and the work you’re considering. We’ll confirm the scope and quote before you decide.

How long will the work take?

Timing depends on the service and required materials. Standard menu delivery is within 48 hours after complete menu materials are received and validated. Other services, custom projects, and missing materials require an agreed delivery date.

What does the Online Menu + QR package include?

Up to 3 menu pages, a QR code, and two revisions. The first year is $144: $129 setup plus $15 hosting. Extra pages cost $20 each. Later hosting is $15/year, arranged separately, with no automatic subscription. Future edits are quoted before work begins.

Can I ask questions before paying?

Yes. Tell us about your project before purchasing if you’re unsure about fit, revisions, hosting, app features, or a custom website.
