Personal brand · Website design

Nino Battaglia

A personal brand website for a graphic designer and illustrator: a bold “A Point of View” homepage, selected work, design services, and a shop for his artwork on shirts.

Visit Nino’s Website ↗

Project focus

Give an artist’s own visual language the lead role, while making his work, services, and shirt shop easy to explore.

What the site includes

  • A homepage led by the headline “A Point of View” and a collage of Nino’s artwork.
  • Selected work and design services: branding and identity, graphic design, illustration, and apparel graphics.
  • A shirt shop with his artwork on apparel, a “Which Nino shirt are you?” quiz, and a cart.
  • Clear routes to contact Nino and discuss a project.

Design choices

A dark, high-contrast layout with large type and a warm orange accent lets the artwork stand out instead of acting as decoration. The same look and navigation carry across desktop and mobile.
