What you get

An app guests keep

  • Your own listing in the App Store and Google Play
  • Menu, specials, hours, call, directions and your ordering link
  • Notifications to every guest who turns them on
  • A rewards card: guests collect a visit with the code at your counter
  • Menu and specials changes reach the app with no store update

How it works

  • After checkout, one short form for your app: name, logo, colours, menu, hours and the reward you want (pre-filled if Menu-Made already has your details).
  • You open developer accounts with Apple ($99 a year, paid to Apple) and Google ($25 once, paid to Google) as your business, and invite Menu-Made. Apple and Google ask for a free D-U-N-S number, which can take a few days to get. We email you the steps.
  • We set up the app in both stores, build it, write the store listing and submit it. Part of this setup is done by hand on Apple’s and Google’s websites, because they require it.
  • Apple and Google review the app, usually in 1 to 3 days. Most apps are live within 1 to 3 weeks of checkout, depending on how quickly the accounts are verified.
  • Website & App Care keeps it running: hosting, notifications, the rewards card, store upkeep and the yearly rebuilds Apple and Google require. Free for the first 90 days.

Good to know

Approval is decided by Apple and Google and isn’t guaranteed. If a store asks for changes, we make them and resubmit.

The app lives in your own developer accounts, so it stays yours. If you cancel Website & App Care, the apps stay in your accounts but notifications and rewards stop, and the app shows your web menu.

Online ordering inside the app isn’t included: the app links to the ordering you already use.

Want something simpler? The Guest App installs from your link or QR code, with notifications and rewards too, and needs no store accounts.
