Start with a clear plan

A custom website needs an agreed scope. We use your goals and materials to define the work, rather than attaching a fixed price to an unknown project.

Pages and customer actions

Agree which pages you need and what people should be able to do: explore your work, find business information, contact you, or follow an agreed booking or purchase route.

Brand and content

Confirm the logo, colors, photography, text, and other materials you will provide. Any additional content work or custom functionality belongs in the quote.

Review and delivery

Agree the review steps, delivery timing, and launch responsibilities in writing. A full rebuild, broader redesign, and extra pages are scoped individually.

What to bring to the conversation

  • Your current website, if you have one, and what is or is not working.
  • The pages, features, and customer actions you have in mind.
  • Your available brand assets and content, plus any desired deadline.

Accounts and access

Confirm who will manage the domain, hosting, and any connected accounts, plus how access and handover will work.

Hosting and renewals
