Apps

Apps for your guests

Two ways to put your business on guests’ phones. Both have your menu, notifications and a rewards card. The difference is whether guests find you in the app stores.

Guest App or Mobile App

Starter

Guest App

An app guests add to their home screen from your link or QR code. No app store needed.

  • Menu, specials, hours, call button and your ordering link
  • Notifications (on iPhone, once it’s on the Home Screen)
  • A rewards card and its own QR code for tables and windows

$29/mo+ $299 setup

Premium

Mobile App

Your own app in the App Store and Google Play, under your business’s name. Guests find and install it like any other app.

  • Your own listing in the App Store and Google Play
  • Notifications to every guest who turns them on
  • A rewards card, plus your menu, specials, hours and ordering link

$999one-time setup

Then Mobile App Care at $99/mo, free for the first 90 days. Store approval is decided by Apple and Google.
